KENNIX STORE STARTER — READ THIS FIRST

What is included:
- Responsive fashion-storefront layout
- Product category filters and sorting
- Shopping bag/cart saved in the browser
- Demo product cards and NGN prices
- No payment credentials or live payment processing

IMPORTANT:
This is a front-end prototype, not a finished live shop. Product art and products are placeholders. Replace them with your actual product photos, names, prices and stock. The checkout button intentionally does not take money.

For real online payment:
1. Create and verify a merchant account with a supported payment provider (for example Paystack or Flutterwave).
2. We must connect a secure checkout flow. Never put secret API keys inside app.js or any browser-visible file.
3. Test successful, failed and cancelled payments before launch.
4. Add order storage, stock management, delivery details, privacy/return policies and order notifications.

FREE HOSTING NOTE:
Some free hosting plans restrict commercial use. Before publishing a store that is taking real orders, check the host's current terms for commercial/e-commerce use. A free subdomain may be available; a custom .com domain usually costs money.

How to edit:
- Product names, categories and sample prices are near the top of app.js.
- Main content and brand name are in index.html.
- Colors and layout are in styles.css.
