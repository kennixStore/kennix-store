📘 KENNIX Admin Guide

Use this guide whenever you need help managing the store. Work through the steps in order.

1. Upload a product

  1. Open Upload Products.
  2. Enter the product name and choose the correct category.
  3. Write a clear description and add relevant specifications.
  4. Enter the original price if applying the 13% discount, or untick the discount option to enter a normal selling price.
  5. Choose a product picture and tap Upload Product.
  6. Open Products to confirm the listing appears correctly.

2. Add colours, sizes or specifications

  1. Open Colors & Sizes.
  2. For clothes, shoes and bags, use the first form and choose Colour, Size, Material, Style or Design.
  3. For phones, accessories and appliances, use the second form and enter the specification name and value, such as Storage: 128GB or Wattage: 1.5kW.
  4. Choose the product, enter available quantity and optionally set a special price.
  5. Tap the save button and check the Existing Stock Options list.

3. Review and confirm customer payments

  1. Open Orders and find the customer's order.
  2. Check the order total and open the uploaded receipt using View Payment Receipt.
  3. Only after confirming the money has actually arrived in the KENNIX account, tap Approve Payment.
  4. If the receipt is incorrect or payment cannot be confirmed, tap Reject Receipt and enter a clear reason.
  5. Never approve a payment based only on a screenshot or customer claim.

4. Process and deliver an order

  1. After payment is confirmed, prepare or source the product.
  2. Change the order status to Processing while preparing it.
  3. Change it to Shipped once it has actually been dispatched.
  4. Change it to Delivered only after delivery is confirmed.
  5. Use Cancelled only when the order has genuinely been cancelled.

5. Manage products

  1. Open Products to view current listings.
  2. Use Edit Product to correct product details or prices.
  3. Use Delete Product only when the listing should be permanently removed.
  4. Check product options and stock before confirming availability to a customer.

6. Approve customer reviews

  1. Open Reviews.
  2. Read the customer's rating and comment.
  3. Tap Approve review to publish an appropriate review.
  4. Delete spam, abusive or irrelevant submissions.

7. Use the Overview

Open Overview to check orders, pending and confirmed payments, revenue, fulfilment progress, active products and low-stock options. Tap Refresh Statistics to reload the figures.

Important KENNIX rules

  • Verify money in the bank account before approving any payment.
  • Do not mark an order as shipped or delivered before it happens.
  • Do not promise stock or delivery dates unless confirmed.
  • Handle customer names, phone numbers, addresses and receipts privately.
  • Both admins currently use the same login, so the system cannot tell which person performed an action. Do not share the password outside the two authorised admins.
