FOUNDER PROFILE

Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy

CEO & Founder of KENNIX

Meet the Founder

Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy, also known as Eze Kenechukwu or Kenny, is a Nigerian entrepreneur and the CEO and Founder of KENNIX, a Nigerian online lifestyle, fashion and electronics retail brand. Through KENNIX, he is working to build a convenient online shopping destination offering customers a growing selection of fashion, technology and everyday products.

Education and Location

Kenny is a student of Theatre and Film Studies at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN). He is currently based in Nsukka, Enugu State, Nigeria, where he pursues his studies. Nsukka is his current location and place of study; it is not being presented here as the headquarters of KENNIX.

About KENNIX

Founded in 2026, KENNIX is an online retail brand offering a growing range of clothing, shoes, bags, caps, fashion accessories, phones, electronics and everyday lifestyle essentials. The brand aims to bring different product categories together in one convenient online destination for customers in Nigeria.

KENNIX's brand tagline is “Your World. Your Style. Your Choice.” The store also uses the slogan “Shop Like a Billionaire.”

Founder’s Role

As CEO and Founder, Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy leads the vision and development of KENNIX, including its brand direction, product range, customer experience, digital operations and business partnerships.
