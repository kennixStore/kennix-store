Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy

CEO & Founder of KENNIX

About Kenny

Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy, popularly known as Kenny, is a Nigerian entrepreneur and the founder of KENNIX. He is focused on building the brand and developing its place in online retail.

About KENNIX

KENNIX is an online retail brand founded to bring a range of products together in one shopping destination. Its product categories include fashion, shoes, bags, caps, phones, accessories, electronics and everyday lifestyle essentials.

The brand's tagline is “Your World. Your Style. Your Choice.” Its focus is to give customers access to a broad selection of useful products through a convenient online shopping experience.

Founder’s focus

As CEO and Founder, Kenny leads the direction and development of KENNIX, with a focus on brand growth, product range, customer experience, supplier relationships and business development.
