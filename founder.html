Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy

About Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy

Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy is a Nigerian entrepreneur and the Founder and CEO of KENNIX, a digital commerce brand created to make fashion, technology products and everyday essentials available through one online shopping destination. He is also known as Eze Kenechukwu and Kenny.

Driven by a vision to bring aspirational retail together with practical everyday products, Kennedy founded KENNIX in 2026. The brand's product interests span contemporary clothing, footwear and accessories, alongside mobile phones, electronics and connectivity-related products.

Education and Location

Alongside his entrepreneurial work, Kennedy studies Theatre and Film Studies at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN). He is currently based in Nsukka, Enugu State, Nigeria, where he pursues his studies. This identifies his current study location and does not designate Nsukka as KENNIX's headquarters.

About KENNIX

Founded in 2026 by Eze Kenechukwu Kennedy, KENNIX is a Nigerian online retail brand offering fashion, electronics and everyday lifestyle essentials. Its range includes clothing, shoes, bags, caps and accessories, with an expanding interest in phones, phone accessories, power banks, Wi-Fi devices and related technology products.

KENNIX's brand tagline is “Your World. Your Style. Your Choice.” The store also uses the slogan “Shop Like a Billionaire.”

Founder’s Role

As Founder and CEO, Kennedy guides KENNIX's vision and development, including its brand direction, product selection, customer experience, digital commerce operations and business partnerships.
